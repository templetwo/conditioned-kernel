## 1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT

The frozen system is an unusually honest **planning machine** wrapped around a scientifically **vacuous primary contrast**. The freeze/ledger/admission machinery is real: the manifest is byte-reproducible by a third party, every exclusion is ledgered with hashes and true reasons, the gates that exist fail closed, all frozen artifacts stamp `unratified` / `dry_planning_only` / `scientific_completion=false`, and no artifact anywhere claims an efficacy result. The audit found **no concealment of execution** — no model ran, provably.

But the scientific object the machinery would execute cannot measure what its frozen label says it measures. Executed evidence: the sole included task's gold equals its entire closed universe (zero in-universe distractors — three of five scorer penalty classes structurally unreachable); the packet contract compiles the complete answer specification into the **control** packets (an answer mechanically extracted from the C1 packet's own bytes scores 1.0); C3's distinguishing content *is the gold answer verbatim* in output-ready form, so the primary contrast is "answer visible vs answer derivable," not "structured continuity vs flat"; no preregistered estimand, direction, decision metric, or outcome-licensing statement exists anywhere; and the two recorded candidate metrics provably disagree on the winner in realizable scenarios. Every possible M0 outcome is pre-hedged in the docs; no result could touch the thesis. Under the brief's own rule (A.6), that mandates BLOCK on the science.

**M0 must not be authorized on this manifest.** The honest classification of any run under it is an end-to-end commissioning run of the instrument, and even that should carry corrected labels. The machinery deserves commissioning; the science requires a new task corpus and a preregistration layer that do not yet exist.

## 3. STRONGEST ARGUMENT THAT THE SYSTEM WORKS

The frozen artifacts are structurally honest to an unusual degree, and this was verified rather than assumed: the manifest is bit-reproducible from a clean interpreter (recomputed 18/1/17 split, byte-identical exclusion ledger, exact hash round-trip); every one of 17 exclusions carries hashes and reasons that spot-check true against the raw task files; `authorization_status=unratified`, `execution_scope=dry_planning_only`, `experiment_contract_id=null`, `scientific_completion=false`, `headline_eligible=false` are stamped at every layer; every injected failure (missing cell, timeout, duplicate, forged unplanned cell, wrong-hash receipt, provenance gap) landed fail-loud or fail-visible in the paths that exist; null scores are never imputed; failures stay in the denominator; and grep-proof exists that no model executed under any M0 artifact. Nothing in the repo claims this M0 tests the founding thesis. By the audit's own principle, most weakness is disclosed — the machinery layer largely earns its labels.

## 4. STRONGEST ARGUMENT THAT THE SYSTEM DOES NOT TEST ITS CLAIMED CONSTRUCT

At exact byte parity, the entire content difference of the primary contrast is the gold answer. C3's `accepted_relations` equal the task's `expected_relations` verbatim, in the scorer's exact key shape, as the first field of the packet; C1's matching bytes are `<<CK_PAD>>` plus 219 spaces. Meanwhile the annotation forces the universes, the `all_required` semantics, and the "return every supported assertion" objective into the C1/C2 packets as REQUIRED_OPERATIONAL_STATE — and because gold equals the full universe cross-product, a mechanical copy of the control packet's own bytes scores 1.0 / exact-match (executed). The frozen label "structured substrate continuity under equal finalized request-byte length" describes a manipulation that was not built. The leakage control scans for a synthetic sentinel that no packet path can ever emit. The repo's own 00.6C receipt records the model's actual prior behavior (a single assertion) that the new conjunctive gold re-scores from full to half credit — making a C3-favoring difference predictable from task construction before any model runs, via copy-vs-construct. No frozen document discloses any of this. That is weakness laundered through rigorous-looking closed-set vocabulary — the exact failure class this codebase names and hunts.

## 5. CONFIRMED IMPLEMENTATION DEFECTS (all reproduced by execution or static proof at 36e889e)

**Blocking-for-any-future-M0 (enforcement gaps at the trust boundary):**
1. `evaluate_admission` never calls `verify_manifest_hash`; `manifest_integrity_ok` = field-presence truthiness. A tampered manifest with a stale hash sails through as "ratified, 100% coverage, integrity ok" (executed; sharpest single violation — trace 13). Doc claims otherwise (admission contract condition 12). One-line fix.
2. Authorization receipt binds to the manifest's embedded hash *label*, not canonical bytes; receipt content (`authorized_model`, cell count) never cross-checked; "ratified_receipt_present" mintable by any well-shaped dict.
3. Terminal ledger is memory-only (no persistence anywhere in `m0_ledger_integration.py`); `retries=0` / `no_replacement_runs` unenforceable across processes — a crash-and-rerun silently builds a clean-looking ledger from replacement runs (executed, trace 20).
4. `terminalize` never binds score records to cells: C1's cell accepted C3's score record (executed, trace 2) — silent score transposition between the arms of the primary contrast is possible. It also accepts `classification=SCORED` with a null score record ("scored completion that did not happen," trace 3) and silently **overwrites** the planned cell's frozen `expected_relation_hash` with whatever the score record carries (trace 5).
5. Packet/control verification statuses on terminal records are caller-attested free strings, never derived from receipts; `control_receipt_hash` optional and unchecked; a pair with a real FAIL receipt was admitted VALID on self-reported "pass" strings (executed, trace 7).
6. No model digest is stored, required, resolved, or admission-checked anywhere; cell identity is digest-blind. The "model fixed" premise has no enforcement point (trace 9).
7. Manifest `runtime_requirements` declares `no_silent_parameter_fallback=true` and `failure_on_unhonored_option=RUNTIME_PROVENANCE_FAILURE` — zero implementing code; the failure code is not a member of the terminal classification enum. A silently ignored seed/temperature/num_ctx is undetectable by construction (trace 19).
8. `EdgeProfile.from_dict` falsy-`or` defaults replace `temperature=0.0`→0.3 and `seed=0`→42: the frozen M0 generation values are unrepresentable through the repo's only options-carrying execution path (executed).

10. The raw-output→scorer extraction path is unwritten, and its two candidate components disagree: `score_cell([])` = SCORED 0.0 vs `parse_continuity_candidate` = SCHEMA_FAILED null. The experiment's most likely small-model failure mode (empty output) lands on an unpinned fork that decides whether the primary pair exists (traces 6/18). `MALFORMED_ASSERTIONS` has no producer.

13. PASS control receipts emit `headline_eligible: true` (the only artifact in the system that ever does), contradicting the repo-wide always-false invariant; the guard checks only `scientific_completion`.

## 6. CONFIRMED SCIENTIFIC-DESIGN DEFECTS

1. **Gold saturates the universe** (D-blocking): expected_relations = the full 1×2×1 cross-product; zero in-universe distractors; WRONG_RELATION / UNSUPPORTED_ASSERTION / REVERSED_DIRECTION unreachable; attainable score space {0, 0.5, 1.0}; assert-everything scores perfect. The scorer's advertised shotgun-resistance is vacuous on the only task it would run.
2. **Answer in the control arms** (B-blocking / D-blocking): the C1/C2 packets carry the complete answer specification by contract; C3 carries the answer verbatim. Zero information asymmetry; the primary contrast measures copy-vs-construct formatting, which `RE_GROUNDING.md` §184-188 already identifies as a small-model formatting phenomenon.
3. **No preregistration** (A-blocking): no estimand, no direction, no threshold, no deciding metric (primary_score vs exact_relation_set_match provably disagree on the winner), no statement of what any outcome licenses; protocol thresholds are marked PROPOSED with no recorded sign-off; docs pre-hedge every direction — no outcome can challenge the thesis (A.6 → BLOCK).
4. **Selection validity** (B): eligibility rule's marker phrases quote the successor prompt's own wording; rule, task, and manifest authored in one commit, by the build seat, after observing predecessor model output and scorer behavior. Fails closed, so it doesn't inflate scores — but "included by rule" and "wrote the rule around the task" are currently indistinguishable.
5. **State-referential prompt vs static gold** (B): episode_b asks what is "still accepted" (a replay property) but gold is static; the only realized Episode-A state on record contained one of the two gold relations — a state-faithful C3 answer would score 0.5 while C1's universe-copy scores 1.0, actively penalizing the treatment for using its treatment.
6. **Predictable direction** (D): the 00.6C prior output plus the choose-one→all_required gold change makes a C3>C1 delta derivable from construction; undisclosed.
7. **No error model** (D): no A/A, scrambled-state, or negative-control cell; a single deterministic point difference cannot be distinguished from a pipeline artifact.
8. **Protocol drift, undisclosed** (C/A): M0's C1 is a state-absent byte-parity control while EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md and RE_GROUNDING.md define C1 as "same state mass, unstructured dump" — different causal questions; three inconsistent live definitions of "M0" (protocol, buoyancy map, manifest spec) with no supersession note; the "permanent" broken-packet instrument arm and echo-degeneracy gate declared ladder-wide are silently absent from the frozen condition set. This is also the README/thesis drift finding for this round: the drift is not in the manifest's stated goal but in unreconciled control semantics across the governing docs.
9. **Under-frozen plan** (C): the planned C3 cell pins no state hash and no byte target — two different Episode-A outcomes yield different C3 packets and pair budgets under the same frozen cell_id (demonstrated 2321 vs 2104 bytes).

## 7. ONE-TASK MANIFEST ADEQUACY RULING

**Inadequate for any scientific claim; adequate only for commissioning.** With n=1 task, 1 replicate, 0 retries, deterministic decoding, the maximum output is one 5-valued delta on a task whose score space is {0, 0.5, 1.0} — an instrument reading, not an estimate. And this particular task is not merely small: it is structurally incapable of carrying the labeled construct (defects 6.1/6.2). One operational failure in either primary cell permanently nulls the manifest (correct, disclosed, and appropriate for commissioning strictness).

## 8. C3-VERSUS-C1 CAUSAL-IDENTIFICATION RULING

**Not identified.** The mechanical byte-parity contract is genuinely enforced (verified at construction: 2321==2321, fail-closed paths) — but byte equality is not information equality. As instantiated, C3 vs C1 is "gold answer present in output-ready form" vs "gold answer derivable from a spec string beside inert padding." Any delta in either direction is unattributable to structured substrate continuity. Additional unflagged confounds: condition names in-band; `<<CK_PAD>>` only in C1; C1's semantic redefinition vs protocol. C2 (planned) de-confounds padding but not the answer-presence asymmetry — C2's body equals C1's, so C3-vs-C2 is equally leaked.

## 9. SCORER VALIDITY RULING

**Internally valid, externally vacuous on this corpus.** The arithmetic does what it claims: primary_score is provably Jaccard similarity over unique canonical triples (200/200 randomized trials), shotgun-monotone, duplicate- and symmetric-aware, null-on-failure with no imputation. Defects are at the boundary: the deciding metric for the primary pair is unpinned (the two recorded metrics disagree on realizable outcomes); the extraction path feeding it is unwritten with divergent empty-answer semantics; and on the sole included task, three of five penalty classes and most of the score range are unreachable — undisclosed in any frozen artifact. `exact_relation_set_match` would be the more defensible primary outcome for a one-pair deterministic run *if* the task were fixed; freezing that choice is required either way.

## 10. FAILURE-ACCOUNTING RULING

**Sound in-process, unenforced across the trust boundary.** Denominator = planned_n always; failures retained as nulls; duplicates and unplanned cells rejected in-session; the 100% pair gate is hard and prevents complete-case bias. But: admission is an accounting of *claims* (self-attested dicts), not evidence — it verifies no hash it is handed; the ledger's exactly-once invariant is process-local; and per-condition failure stratification (a substrate-relevant outcome under the thesis) is absent from the report schema. The one-shot fragility (F.8: any primary-cell failure permanently nulls the manifest) is real, disclosed, and appropriate for commissioning; it also means a small local model may structurally never produce a headline — acceptable for M0, worth a replicate policy in any post-M0 design.

## 11. PROVENANCE RULING

**Overstated by the manifest; under-implemented by the code.** Model identity is a mutable tag with no digest anywhere in the chain; runtime requirements are declared with zero enforcement and an unrepresentable failure code; generation parameters on terminal records restate the plan rather than record observation; no response bytes or hashes retained; load-state bimodality (the repo's own DETERMINISM.md finding) unpinned at 1 replicate; `repository_commit` names a commit that cannot rebuild the manifest; and the manifest-hash convention itself is documented only in code. The honest current label for the provenance block is "declared, unimplemented, gate required before execution."
