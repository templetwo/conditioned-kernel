## LN-1 — H2 MEASUREMENT FLOOR
At n=10 the 95% interval on 7/10 runs ~0.40–0.89. "Approaching" is undecidable below roughly a 40-point difference. Pooling five kernels gives n=50 but assumes exchangeability across a deliberately heterogeneous set (crc32 closed, median3x3 open), so it is not recommended as primary.
THE SHARPER FLOOR, and it is on YOUR endpoint: D is the mean over probes of (1 − largest cluster fraction) among ACCEPTED artifacts. With k accepted artifacts the largest-cluster fraction is a multiple of 1/k, so per-probe disagreement is QUANTIZED TO STEPS OF 1/k. At k=10 the smallest non-zero per-probe disagreement is 0.1; at k=4 it is 0.25.
Consequence: D's resolution is bounded by ACCEPTANCE COUNT, not by the 256 probes. More probes refine the mean, not the quantum. And acceptance rate and D-precision are coupled — the weak generators H2 most cares about are exactly the ones whose D is least well estimated. That is a structural asymmetry in the instrument, not a property of the models.

## LN-2 — SHARED PRIORS, AND THE PART THAT SHOULD WORRY YOU
The four generators are not independent draws. These are canonical textbook kernels; a reflected CRC-32 with poly 0xEDB88320 appears in training corpora thousands of times in near-identical form. Two generators can produce byte-divergent, behaviorally identical artifacts because they MEMORIZED THE SAME IMPLEMENTATION, independent of whether the ECS pinned anything.
Direction: shared priors DEFLATE D. Two opposite-signed consequences:
  ON H1 — CONSERVATIVE. Deflation at both completeness levels compresses dynamic range and attenuates the predicted dose-response rise. A surviving H1 result is stronger than it looks; an H1 null is weaker evidence of absence, because attenuation is a live alternative explanation.
  ON THE CALIBRATION GATE — ANTI-CONSERVATIVE. This is the serious one.
The gate assumes disagreement on crc32 would be caused ONLY by harness leakage. But crc32 is the MOST MEMORIZED KERNEL IN THE SET, so generators converge on it whether or not the harness is clean. A REAL HARNESS LEAK CAN BE MASKED BY MEMORIZATION AND THE GATE PASSES ANYWAY.
The gate keeps power in one direction only:
  FAIL (D > 1%) → still highly informative, halting is correct.
  PASS (D ≤ 1%) → NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT. It licenses only "no leak large enough to overcome strong shared priors on the most canonical kernel available." It does NOT license "the harness is leak-free."
