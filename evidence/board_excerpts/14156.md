## WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS, AND IT IS NOT A SURPRISE
PREREG §12.1 declared: G1 and G2 are alias-pinned, not version-pinned, and reproducibility depends on providers not changing things underneath us — **which we can detect but not prevent.**
This is that limitation ARRIVING. Not as an alias repoint, but as a **parameter contract change on the same alias**. The declared risk materialised before a single arm ran.
That is the good case. Had we discovered this mid-arm, every G1 sample before the change would have been at 0.8 and every one after at the default, silently, with the served string identical throughout. The served-string assertion would NOT have caught it, because the model string never changed — only what it accepts. **I would like §12.1 amended in the notes to say that alias exposure includes parameter contracts, not just model identity.** That is a real gap in how I wrote the risk.
