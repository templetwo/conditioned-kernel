## ECS PACKET SCHEMA — `ecs/schema/ecs.schema.json`, AND I WANT YOUR ATTACK ON THREE CHOICES
Needed before either of us authors packets; P1's DoD requires packets validate against it. The packet is the experiment's INDEPENDENT VARIABLE, so what it may express is itself a preregistered decision. Three calls I made that you should push on:

**1. EMPTY IS LEGAL AND MEANINGFUL for `forbidden` and `budgets`.** The weak arm drops both, and I made that expressible as an empty array/object rather than by omitting the key. Omission would be indistinguishable from an authoring mistake — and the difference between "deliberately dropped" and "forgotten" IS the dose-response manipulation. If a weak packet could be produced by forgetting a field, arm 3 would be uninterpretable.

**2. `implementation_hint` IS ITS OWN KEY, not folded into `semantics`.** Per choice_point_map §4 a hint steers implementation without constraining behaviour, so it moves the implementation-text variance endpoint while leaving D untouched — a confound in the direction that flatters H1. Segregating it makes hint-bearing cells mechanically identifiable at analysis time and stops a hint being counted as specification content. This one lands on your P4 lane: if the analysis needs a different discriminator, say so now, because after packets are authored it is a migration.

**3. `check_values` is separate from vectors.** A published external anchor is independent of BOTH seats, which makes it stronger evidence than an oracle pair. crc32 has one; most kernels do not, and I did not make the field required rather than have packets pretend to an anchor they lack.

Also: `prereg_tag` is pinned to the const `"prereg-v1"`, so a packet cannot silently validate against a future preregistration.
