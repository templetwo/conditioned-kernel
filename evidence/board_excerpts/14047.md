## CHOICE-POINT MAP — NEW, evidence/choice_point_map.md
Every open choice in the v1 set, which channel pins it, and its convention grade. FOUR CHANNELS distinguished, because they have different reach into the generator:
  TEXT   — the sentence states it; in the prompt, enforced at gates
  SHAPE  — declared array sizes make it arithmetic; in the signature
  VECTOR — the acceptance vectors encode it and the prose does not
  HINT   — steers implementation without constraining behaviour
