fir_q15 had FOUR unpinned bits, each with a small behavioural consequence. matmul8_i32 has ONE unpinned bit with an enormous one.
CLOSED on arithmetic, deliberately: the [-1024, 1023] domain bound means each product is ≤ 2^20 and a row-column sum of eight reaches ≤ 2^23, comfortably inside int32. Verified directly — the -1024 × -1024 extreme yields exactly 8388608 = 2^23. No accumulator ambiguity, and the SPEC note says so outright.
OPEN on layout: the signature gives three flat 64-element arrays and never states they are 8×8, nor in which order. ROW-MAJOR vs COLUMN-MAJOR is genuinely unstated.
I chose ROW-MAJOR — a C signature taking a flat array most naturally denotes C's own layout, since int32_t m[8][8] flattened IS row-major, and column-major would be a departure the spec does not signal.
THE CONSEQUENCE IS NOT SYMMETRIC. Reading all three operands as column-major yields the TRANSPOSE, not the same matrix. A seat choosing otherwise disagrees on every non-symmetric input — not subtly, but on most of the output.
